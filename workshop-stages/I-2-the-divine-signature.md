# I-2. The Divine Signature

## Invitation
Who does God say I am before I do anything?

## What we are trying to surface
- Identity received, not earned
- The difference between doctrine and lived assurance
- The participant's actual hearing of grace

## Questions tab format

- Present each core question with multiple-choice options where possible.
- Include `None of these fit me yet` and `Other (my own words)` in each set.
- After selection, ask for one short concrete example before moving on.

## Exercise: God's banquet

Ask the participant to imagine being invited to God's banquet and to place themselves in the scene.

Keep it simple: have them point, sketch, or place a mark in the scene before they interpret it.

## Core questions

### Q1. When you hear the words "beloved," "chosen," or "adopted," what in you resists them?

Options:
- I feel unworthy
- I fear presumption
- I distrust emotional language
- I expect disappointment
- I feel numb to those words
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q2. What do you still act as if you have to prove?

Options:
- Competence
- Moral strength
- Spiritual consistency
- Usefulness
- Independence
- Lovability
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q3. Where do you live as though acceptance is conditional?

Options:
- When I fail

## Follow-up probes
- What makes that hard to believe in practice?
- When does that truth disappear from view?
- What do you do when it does?
- Which part feels true in theory but not in your body?
- What would living from that truth look like on a hard day?

## Move from abstraction to truth
- Ask for a recent moment of failure or shame.
- Ask what they assumed God thought of them then.
- Ask what they actually did afterward.

## What truth sounds like
- Acknowledgement of fear, not polished doctrine.
- Language that shows where grace is resisted.
- Honest tension between belief and habit.
