# P-3. What Breaks Your Heart

## Invitation
What injustice or need keeps finding me?

## What we are trying to surface
- The burden that keeps reappearing
- What the participant cannot ignore
- The point where compassion, grief, and attention meet

## Questions tab format

- Present each core question with multiple-choice options where possible.
- Include `None of these fit me yet` and `Other (my own words)` in each set.
- After selection, ask for one short concrete example before moving on.

## Core questions

### Q1. What kind of pain or injustice keeps drawing your attention?

Options:
- Loneliness and disconnection
- High-performing people quietly losing themselves
- Young men growing up without steady guidance
- Unequal access shaped by postcode, class, or system design
- Family breakdown
- Poverty or material vulnerability
- Spiritual confusion
- Church hurt
- Injustice in systems or institutions
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q2. What do you feel compelled to act on, even when it is inconvenient?

Options:
- Listening deeply to people in pain
- Practical help
- Advocacy or speaking up
- Prayer and spiritual support
- Mentoring or discipling
- Creating safer spaces for others
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q3. What problem do you keep noticing that other people seem to miss?

Options:
- Hidden burnout
- Quiet despair
- Relational fragmentation
- Spiritual performative culture
- Lack of practical follow-through
- Exclusion of vulnerable people
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q4. What do you grieve that others may have normalised?

Options:
- Contempt in public speech
- Emotional numbness
- Treating people as output
- Cynicism as wisdom
- Spiritual shallowness
- Isolation as normal life
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q5. What part of the world leaves you unable to stay detached?

Options:
- Young adults and students
- Women carrying leadership and disappearing inside it
- Boys and men without stable presence or guidance
- People affected by structural inequity in care or opportunity
- Families and parenting pressures
- People in transition or crisis
- Church communities in conflict
- Workplaces under strain
- People with little support network
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

## Follow-up probes
- Tell me about a recent moment when that hit you hard.
- What did you do with that feeling?
- Where does your attention keep returning?
- What are you angry or sad enough about to stay with it?
- What need feels personal, not abstract?

## Move from abstraction to truth
- Ask for a concrete encounter, not a cause in general.
- Ask what they actually did next.
- Ask what they keep carrying in memory.

## What truth sounds like
- Real grief or concern.
- Specific suffering, not generic compassion.
- A burden that has already shaped behaviour.
