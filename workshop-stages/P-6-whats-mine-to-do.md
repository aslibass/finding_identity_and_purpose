# P-6. What's Mine to Do

## Invitation
What is the one obedient step I'm being invited into?

## What we are trying to surface
- One concrete act of obedience
- The difference between a calling and a vague aspiration
- A next step that is small enough to do and serious enough to matter

## Questions tab format

- Present each core question with multiple-choice options where possible.
- Include `None of these fit me yet` and `Other (my own words)` in each set.
- After selection, ask for one short concrete example before moving on.

## Exercise: Commit to one next step

Ask the participant to name one concrete action, one date, and one person who will know about it.

If they hesitate, start with a one-sentence commitment and refine the date and witness after.

If they still cannot name an action, date, and witness, do not move on until they can.

### Exercise tab instructions

1. Enter one action.
2. Enter one date.
3. Enter one witness name.
4. Continue only when all three fields are complete.

## Core questions

### Q1. What is the next faithful step in front of you, and by what date will you do it?

Options:
- Begin one concrete action this week
- Schedule one difficult conversation
- Start one recurring practice
- Stop one competing commitment
- Ask one person for accountability
- I cannot name the step yet
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write the exact action and date.

### Q2. What can you do in the next seven days that would count as obedience?

Options:
- Have one honest conversation
- Make one decision I have delayed
- Commit one hour to focused action
- Confess one hidden issue
- Set one boundary and communicate it
- Ask for prayer and support
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent or scheduled example (1-3 sentences).

### Q3. What would make this step real rather than theoretical?

Options:
- Calendar it
- Tell a witness
- Break it into first small task
- Set a deadline with consequence
- Remove one barrier now
- Track completion visibly
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent or scheduled example (1-3 sentences).

### Q4. What support or constraint do you need to move?

Options:
- Accountability partner
- Time block and protected focus
- Clear boundaries
- Practical resources
- Emotional encouragement
- Spiritual covering and prayer
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent or scheduled example (1-3 sentences).

### Q5. What are you prepared to say yes to, and what must you leave behind?

Options:
- Yes to obedience, no to delay
- Yes to truth, no to image management
- Yes to service, no to self-protection
- Yes to faithfulness, no to distraction
- Yes to accountability, no to isolation
- I am not yet ready to answer this clearly
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent or scheduled example (1-3 sentences).

## Follow-up probes
- What would delay look like?
- What makes this hard to start?
- Who needs to know?
- What will you put on the calendar?
- How will you know you have actually done it?

## Move from abstraction to truth
- Require a date, name, or action.
- Ask for the smallest meaningful obedient step.
- Ask what gets done first.

## What truth sounds like
- One specific commitment.
- A timeframe.
- A real-world action, not a mood.
