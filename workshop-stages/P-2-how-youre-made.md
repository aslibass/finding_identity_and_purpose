# P-2. How You're Made

## Invitation
What has God built into me that energises rather than depletes?

## What we are trying to surface
- Natural strengths that feel alive rather than forced
- Patterns of energy, effectiveness, and ease
- What the participant consistently returns to

## Questions tab format

- Present each core question with multiple-choice options where possible.
- Include `None of these fit me yet` and `Other (my own words)` in each set.
- After selection, ask for one short concrete example before moving on.

Test the result by asking how it serves others, what fruit it produces, and whether it leads to concrete obedience.

## Exercise: Find your sweet spot

Ask the participant to draw three circles: talent, skills, and passion. Then ask them to name the overlap that feels most alive and most trustworthy.

Keep the drawing rough and fast: three circles and a few words are enough.

### Exercise tab instructions

1. Draw three circles: talent, skills, and passion.
2. Fill each circle with short phrases.
3. Name at least one phrase in the overlap and continue.

### Visual example

Talent: teaching, listening

Skills: facilitation, writing

Passion: spiritual formation, mentoring

Overlap: guiding reflective conversations

## Follow-up probes
- What do you do without needing to hype yourself up?
- What drains you even when you are good at it?
- Where do people trust you more than you trust yourself?
- What do you repeatedly return to when nobody directs you?
- What part of you is useful before it is impressive?

## Move from abstraction to truth
- Ask about energy, not just skill.
- Ask about repetition, not just one-off talent.
- Ask for examples of flow and depletion.

## What truth sounds like
- A grounded list of lived strengths.
- No inflated gift language.
- Clear distinction between delight and duty.
