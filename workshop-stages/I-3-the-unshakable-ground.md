# I-3. The Unshakable Ground

## Invitation
What does it mean that God is near by promise, not feeling?

## What we are trying to surface
- The participant's attachment to emotional certainty
- What sustains faith when feeling is absent
- The difference between God's nearness and inner experience

## Questions tab format

- Present each core question with multiple-choice options where possible.
- Include `None of these fit me yet` and `Other (my own words)` in each set.
- After selection, ask for one short concrete example before moving on.

## Exercise: Draw your image of God

Ask the participant to draw their relationship with God as simply as they can. The goal is not artistic skill; it is to surface how God is experienced in the participant's imagination and body.

Encourage the smallest possible sketch: shapes, stick figures, distance, or light are enough.

Treat the drawing as diagnostic, not definitive. It is a starting point for discernment, not a revelation in itself.

### Exercise tab instructions

1. Draw your image of God with the smallest sketch that feels honest.
2. Show distance and placement (near, far, above, behind, inside, outside).
3. Continue once the sketch includes both you and God.

## Core questions

- Other (my own words)

## Follow-up probes
- Tell me about a season when feeling failed you.
- What helped you continue then?
- What did you stop doing because it did not feel rewarding?
- What did you learn about God in that season?
- What do you still not trust when the experience is dry?

## Move from abstraction to truth
- Ask about a recent week, not a general season.
- Ask what they did when prayer felt flat.
- Ask what they believed about God anyway.

## What truth sounds like
- Concrete habits of faith.
- Less performance, more perseverance.
- Naming the temptation to measure God by sensation.
