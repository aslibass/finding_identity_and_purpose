# I-4. Lies & True Names

## Invitation
What false stories do I carry - and what does God say instead?

## What we are trying to surface
- The participant's recurring self-deceptions
- Old labels that still govern behavior
- The counter-truth that can name them without denial

## Questions tab format

- Present each core question with multiple-choice options where possible.
- Include `None of these fit me yet` and `Other (my own words)` in each set.
- After selection, ask for one short concrete example before moving on.

## Core questions

### Q1. What do you most often accuse yourself of being?

Options:
- Not enough
- Too much
- A failure
- Unreliable
- Unlovable
- Replaceable
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q2. What lie do you return to when you are tired, rejected, or ashamed?

Options:
- I am on my own
- I have to earn love
- If I fail, I am nothing
- I cannot change
- I must stay in control
- I do not belong
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q3. Whose voice taught you that lie?

Options:
- Family environment
- Church culture
- Peer group
- Authority figure
- Repeated experiences, not one person
- My own inner narrative over time
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q4. What do you do because that lie feels true?

Options:
- Overwork
- People-please
- Withdraw
- Defend myself
- Hide struggle
- Control outcomes
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

### Q5. What true name are you least able to receive?

Options:
- Beloved
- Forgiven
- Adopted
- Chosen
- Seen
- Secure
- None of these fit me yet
- Other (my own words)

After selecting, write one recent example (1-3 sentences).

## Follow-up probes
- Where did that story begin?
- What does it cost you to keep believing it?
- How does it show up in your relationships or work?
- What would have to be true for you to let it go?
- What truth feels more frightening than the lie?

## Move from abstraction to truth
- Ask for the exact sentence they hear in their head.
- Ask for a recent incident that triggered it.
- Ask what behavior followed immediately.

## What truth sounds like
- Plain naming of the lie.
- No spiritual bypassing.
- A counter-name that sounds believable enough to practice.
