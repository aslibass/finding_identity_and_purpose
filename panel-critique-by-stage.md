# Panel Critique By Stage

This file records the current expert-panel read of the workshop stage content.

## Overall judgment

The stage set is now coherent, pastorally usable, and much harder to fake than earlier drafts.

- The exercise-first structure lowers activation energy.
- The question design improves recognition before articulation.
- The multiple-choice scaffolding helps participants who lack vocabulary.
- The strongest remaining caution is to keep the Purpose Arc anchored in Scripture, community, and fruit.

## Identity Arc

### I-1. The Honest Inventory
- Strong across all four panel lenses.
- The checklist format surfaces attachment quickly without requiring advanced self-language.
- The second-pass movement toward what remains before God gives the stage theological depth.
- Ongoing caution: facilitators still need to push beyond selected options into real confession and lived evidence.

### I-2. The Divine Signature
- Pastorally strong and accessible.
- The banquet exercise is low-friction and reveals belonging, distance, and posture well.
- The questions on belovedness, proving, and conditional acceptance are clear and usable.
- Ongoing caution: keep the stage grounded in objective grace and adoption, not only self-perception.

### I-3. The Unshakable Ground
- One of the best-balanced stages.
- The drawing is properly treated as diagnostic, not revelatory.
- The emphasis on promise over feeling is biblically and pastorally strong.
- The final promise question anchors the stage well.

### I-4. Lies & True Names
- Sharp and usable.
- The questions name lies clearly and make self-deception easier to recognize.
- The stage resists spiritual bypassing and pushes toward concrete incidents.
- Ongoing caution: the true-name options help, but facilitators should keep the follow-up space open so participants do not feel forced into a label too early.

### I-5. The Shaped Story
- Strong and pastorally intelligent.
- The landscape exercise externalizes story without overinterpreting it.
- The abandonment/absence and recurring-grace language better meets real wounds.
- The stage now holds grief and grace together well.

### I-6. Repentance & Remaking
- Much stronger after the grace guardrail.
- Repentance is clearly framed as response to grace, not self-improvement.
- The stage presses toward practical obedience in a way that is concrete and testable.
- Ongoing caution: facilitators must keep repentance rooted in truth-telling, not just productivity.

## Purpose Arc

### P-1. Values & Convictions
- Strong and clear.
- The ranking exercise helps expose actual priorities under pressure.
- The stage surfaces evidence, not slogans.
- No major panel concerns.

### P-2. How You're Made
- Useful, but still the most vulnerable stage in the set.
- The sweet-spot exercise is now better anchored by service, fruit, and obedience.
- The stage still risks drifting toward self-optimization if facilitated loosely.
- This stage needs the clearest facilitator discipline to keep giftedness tied to stewardship.

### P-3. What Breaks Your Heart
- Substantially improved.
- The revised options now better capture fatherlessness, women disappearing inside leadership, and structural inequity.
- The stage is emotionally honest and more recognisable for different participants.
- Ongoing caution: burden must still move toward responsibility and action, not stop at sorrow.

### P-4. The Convergence
- Now passes the panel's biggest earlier concern.
- Scripture, community, and fruit provide strong discernment checks.
- The stage is much less likely to drift into self-authored meaning.
- Ongoing caution: the calling options are strong enough that some participants may choose a polished answer too quickly unless evidence is pressed.

### P-5. Community & Discernment
- Good and improved.
- The stage now better captures making honest space, consistent presence, and externally named strengths.
- It properly resists solo interpretation of calling.
- No major panel concerns beyond keeping the examples concrete.

### P-6. What's Mine to Do
- One of the strongest stages in the set.
- The action, date, and witness structure produces accountable outcomes.
- The stage does not let conviction remain abstract.
- No major panel concerns.

## Practical conclusion

The Identity Arc is strong and pastorally careful.
The Purpose Arc is strong when Scripture, community, and fruit remain active constraints.

If the app implementation preserves the current structure and does not flatten the stages into a survey flow, the workshop should surface real truth with much less drag than the earlier drafts.
