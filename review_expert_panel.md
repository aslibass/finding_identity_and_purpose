# Review Expert Panel

Use this panel to review workshop content, stage wording, exercises, and any prompt that depends on theology, discernment, or human formation.

## Panel Members

### Biblical Fidelity
- Checks whether the content stays anchored in Scripture and clear theological claims.
- Best at spotting vague spirituality, proof-texting, or content that drifts from the gospel.

### Clarity and Coherence
- Checks whether the language is concrete, readable, and functionally helpful.
- Best at spotting vague wording, overstatement, or prompts that are too abstract to answer honestly.

### Pastoral Sensitivity
- Checks whether the content is safe, wise, and proportionate for the level of vulnerability it asks for.
- Best at spotting prompts that might shame, rush, or overexpose participants.

### Discernment and Fruit
- Checks whether the exercise leads to tested action, observable change, or accountable next steps.
- Best at spotting prompts that feel moving but do not produce concrete fruit.

## Review Questions

- Is this exercise concrete enough to reveal truth instead of polished answers?
- Does this stay biblically grounded, coherent, and pastorally sensitive?
- Does the format lower activation energy while still requiring honesty?
- Does the content produce something observable, testable, or revisitable?
- Does it avoid vague mystical language and generic self-help framing?

## Practical Rule

If the panel disagrees, prefer the option that is more concrete, more biblically grounded, and harder to fake in live facilitation.
