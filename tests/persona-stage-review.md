# Persona Stage Review

This file records the current read of the updated workshop stages against the three test personas in `tests/personas.ts`.

## Overall result

The updated stage design is easier for all three personas than the earlier open-ended version.

- The exercise-first flow lowers activation energy.
- The multiple-choice-first questions reduce vocabulary burden.
- The short concrete example step still pushes answers beyond checkbox recognition.
- The personas reach mostly the same conclusions as before, but with less drift and less polished self-protection.

## Persona conclusions

### Sarah Chen
- Easier now because the options directly surface competence, control, productivity, usefulness, and conditional acceptance.
- Main conclusion is unchanged: identity built on performance; purpose converges around healthcare equity.
- Improved precision: she can recognize her patterns earlier instead of narrating her way into them.

### Marcus Williams
- Easier now because the options surface reputation, proving, fatherlessness, overwork, and loyalty without requiring him to formulate all of that unaided.
- Main conclusion is unchanged: his life points toward consistent presence for fatherless young men.
- Improved precision: the updated wording exposes earlier that the legal career is partly an escape structure.

### Priya Sharma
- Benefits most from the update because the options make it easier to recognize usefulness, invisibility, over-functioning, and identity collapse.
- Main conclusion is unchanged: her purpose still points toward helping women in leadership recover identity and voice.
- Improved precision: she reaches the wound faster, with less elegant self-explanation masking it.

## Remaining wording hotspots

These are the stages that most needed wording refinement for the three personas:

- `I-5` needed stronger language for abandonment/absence and recurring grace through one faithful person.
- `P-3` needed more recognisable wording for fatherlessness, women disappearing inside leadership, and structural inequity.
- `P-5` needed options that better captured making honest space, showing up consistently, and naming strengths others see first.

## Practical conclusion

The updated stages are not changing the personas' destination as much as changing the quality of the path.

The workshop now supports:
- recognition before articulation
- lower-friction honesty
- less dependence on polished self-awareness
- more consistent surfacing across different personality styles
